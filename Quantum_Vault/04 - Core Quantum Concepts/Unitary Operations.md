# Unitary Operations

## Definition
**Unitary Operations** are norm-preserving linear operations represented by unitary matrices $U$ ($U^\dagger U = I$).

## Key Equations
$$\langle U \psi | U \phi \rangle = \langle \psi | \phi \rangle$$
