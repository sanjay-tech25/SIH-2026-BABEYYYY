# Mermin Peres Magic Square

## Problem
Alice and Bob fill a $3 \times 3$ grid with $\pm 1$ values such that grid rows sum to $+1$ (even parity of $-1$s) and columns sum to $-1$ (odd parity of $-1$s).

## Quantum Advantage
Classically impossible ($0\%$ deterministic win). Sharing two Bell pairs allows Alice and Bob to achieve **$100\%$ winning strategy**, demonstrating quantum pseudo-telepathy and quantum contextuality.
