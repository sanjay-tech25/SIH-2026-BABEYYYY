# Quantum Errors

## Definition
**Quantum Errors** occur due to system interaction with the environment, resulting in bit flips ($X$), phase flips ($Z$), or combined bit-phase flips ($Y = iXZ$), as well as non-unitary amplitude damping and phase damping.
