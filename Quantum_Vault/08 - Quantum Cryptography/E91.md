# E91

## Overview
The **Ekert91 (E91)** protocol is an entanglement-based Quantum Key Distribution scheme formulated by Artur Ekert in 1991.

## Mechanism
Alice and Bob receive paired photons from an entangled Bell state $|\Phi^+\rangle$. They measure in random rotated bases. Eavesdropping eavesdroppers disrupt Bell inequality CHSH bounds ($S = 2\sqrt{2}$ drops toward $S \le 2$).
