# IBM Quantum

## Overview
**IBM Quantum** is a leading ecosystem offering cloud access to real superconducting quantum processors, simulation backends, and learning materials.

---

## Key Platforms & Documentation Resources

### 1. IBM Quantum Learning
- **URL**: https://learning.quantum.ibm.com/
- **What It Teaches**: Interactive, structured courses on quantum information theory, Qiskit SDK, variational algorithms, and error mitigation.
- **Notes Supported**: [[Qubit]], [[Quantum Gates]], [[Qiskit]], [[VQE]], [[Grover Algorithm]]

### 2. IBM Quantum Platform & Composer
- **URL**: https://quantum.ibm.com/
- **What It Teaches**: Visual drag-and-drop circuit composer, hardware queue monitoring, and job execution on real IBM Eagle and Heron processors.
- **Notes Supported**: [[Circuit Experiments]], [[Qiskit]]

### 3. Qiskit Documentation
- **URL**: https://docs.quantum.ibm.com/
- **What It Teaches**: API specifications, transpiler passes, Qiskit Runtime primitives (`Sampler`, `Estimator`), and provider integration.
- **Notes Supported**: [[Qiskit]], [[Algorithm Implementations]]
