# Research Papers

## Overview
Landmark historical and modern academic research papers establishing quantum computational complexity advantages and algorithm designs.

---

## Key Foundational Papers

### 1. Deutsch & Jozsa (1992)
- **Title**: *Rapid Solution of Problems by Quantum Computation*
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1992.0167
- **What It Teaches**: Introduces the Deutsch-Jozsa algorithm establishing the first exponential quantum query speedup.
- **Notes Supported**: [[Deutsch-Jozsa Algorithm]], [[Quantum Oracle]]

### 2. Bernstein & Vazirani (1993)
- **Title**: *Quantum Complexity Theory*
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1145/167088.167097
- **What It Teaches**: Formalizes quantum complexity classes (BQP) and presents the Bernstein-Vazirani parity algorithm.
- **Notes Supported**: [[Bernstein-Vazirani Algorithm]]

### 3. Simon (1994)
- **Title**: *On the Power of Quantum Computation*
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1109/SFCS.1994.365700
- **What It Teaches**: Proves exponential query speedup for finding hidden XOR masks, inspiring Shor's algorithm.
- **Notes Supported**: [[Simon Algorithm]], [[Period Finding]]

### 4. Shor (1994)
- **Title**: *Algorithms for Quantum Computation: Discrete Logarithms and Factoring*
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1109/SFCS.1994.365700
- **What It Teaches**: Polynomial-time quantum algorithms for integer factorization and discrete logarithms.
- **Notes Supported**: [[Shor Algorithm]], [[Quantum Fourier Transform]], [[Quantum Phase Estimation]]

### 5. Grover (1996)
- **Title**: *A Fast Quantum Mechanical Algorithm for Database Search*
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1145/237814.237866
- **What It Teaches**: Quadratic speedup for unstructured database search.
- **Notes Supported**: [[Grover Algorithm]], [[Amplitude Amplification]]

### 6. Peruzzo et al. (2014) - VQE
- **Title**: *A variational eigenvalue solver on a photonic quantum processor*
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms5213
- **What It Teaches**: Introduces VQE for NISQ quantum chemistry.
- **Notes Supported**: [[VQE]], [[Quantum State]]

### 7. Farhi, Goldstone, Gutmann (2014) - QAOA
- **Title**: *A Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm*
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.4028
- **What It Teaches**: Formulates QAOA for combinatorial optimization problems.
- **Notes Supported**: [[QAOA]]

### 8. Harrow, Hassidim, Lloyd (2009) - HHL
- **Title**: *Quantum Algorithm for Linear Systems of Equations*
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.103.150502
- **What It Teaches**: Exponential quantum speedup for solving linear system equations $Ax=b$.
- **Notes Supported**: [[HHL]], [[Quantum Phase Estimation]]
