# Quantum Katas

## Overview
The **Microsoft Quantum Katas** is an open-source collection of self-paced programming tutorials and exercises designed to teach quantum computing concepts and quantum algorithms step-by-step.

## Katas Curriculum Topic Coverage

- **Linear Algebra**: Complex numbers, state vectors, operations.
- **Qubits & Gates**: Single-qubit & multi-qubit transformations.
- **Measurements**: Computational basis measurement, state discrimination.
- **Quantum Protocols**: Teleportation, Superdense Coding, CHSH Game.
- **Oracles & Query Algorithms**: Deutsch-Jozsa, Bernstein-Vazirani, Simon.
- **Search Algorithms**: Grover's search, SAT solving, Graph coloring.
- **Phase Estimation & Fourier**: QFT, QPE, Quantum Counting, Period Finding, Shor.
- **Cryptography & QEC**: BB84, Error correction codes.
