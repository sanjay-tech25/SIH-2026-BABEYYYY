# Courses

## Overview
Top university and industry online courses for mastering quantum computation, quantum algorithm design, and linear algebra.

---

## Recommended Curriculum Courses

### 1. MIT OpenCourseWare: Quantum Information Science
- **URL**: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-371j-quantum-information-science-spring-2018/
- **What It Teaches**: Rigorous graduate-level quantum mechanics, density matrices, quantum error correction, and algorithm proof techniques.
- **Notes Supported**: [[Quantum State]], [[Measurement]], [[Entanglement]], [[Shor Algorithm]]

### 2. IBM Quantum Summer School
- **URL**: https://learning.quantum.ibm.com/
- **What It Teaches**: Comprehensive 2-week intensive lecture series covering Qiskit, pulse control, quantum chemistry, and noise mitigation.
- **Notes Supported**: [[Qiskit]], [[VQE]], [[Quantum Phase Estimation]]

### 3. EdX / Caltech: Quantum Cryptography & Algorithms
- **URL**: https://www.edx.org/
- **What It Teaches**: Mathematical foundations of quantum Oracles, Simon's algorithm, Grover search, and Shor's factoring algorithm.
- **Notes Supported**: [[Simon Algorithm]], [[Grover Algorithm]], [[Shor Algorithm]]
